Lessons

Every kind ofwedding dance

Every lesson is private and planned around you. Bring a song you love (or let me help you choose one) and we'll build something that suits your style, your timeline and how confident you feel.

01

Your first dance

A routine choreographed to your song, shaped around your personalities and ability. It can be a gentle sway with one lovely spin or a full showstopper. Either way, it'll feel like you.

02

Parent & family dances

Father and daughter, mother and son, or anyone who means the world to you. A simple, heartfelt routine so you can enjoy the moment instead of worrying about your feet.

03

Group routines

Bridal party surprises, family numbers and fun group routines. Great for getting everyone involved and giving your guests something to talk about for years.

Good questions

Can't see yours here? Just ask, I'm always happy to chat.

We've never danced before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of my couples are complete beginners, and plenty arrive with one partner who's been gently talked into it. We start right at the beginning.

How many lessons will we need?

It depends on your song and how much you'd like to learn. Some couples want something simple, others want a full routine. We'll work it out together when we first chat.

When should we start?

Earlier is always nicer, as it gives you time to relax into it without cramming. I do get booked up, so it's worth getting in touch as soon as you have a date.

Can you help us choose a song?

Of course. If you're torn between a few, bring them along and we'll find the one that feels right to dance to.

What should we wear?

Anything comfortable. For a lesson or two closer to the day, bring the shoes you'll be wearing so you can get used to them.

Let's plan your dance

Get in touch for pricing and availability. Couples often book a few months out, so it's worth saying hello early.
