About

Hi,
I'm Vik!

Dancer, teacher, mum to Carter, and the person who's going to make your first dance feel easy.

my story

Dance has always been how I celebrate, how I unwind and how I connect with people

I started dancing as a little girl and never really stopped. Somewhere along the way I realised the part I loved most wasn't performing, it was helping other people feel that same joy.

Teaching wedding couples is my favourite kind of teaching. Most people arrive a bit nervous, usually with one partner who's been talked into it. Then there's a lesson where someone misses a step, you both burst out laughing, and suddenly you're actually dancing. I live for that moment.

I teach from a private studio in my home near the Auckland Botanic Gardens, so there's no audience and no pressure. And since becoming mum to Carter, I've got even more patience than I used to. You'll never feel rushed here.

What lessons with me feel like

01

Relaxed

A calm, private space. No mirrors full of strangers, no one looking on.

02

Made for you

Your song, your personalities, your comfort level. Nothing forced or over complicated.

03

Genuinely fun

Laughing at the wobbly bits is encouraged. It's meant to be something you look forward to.
